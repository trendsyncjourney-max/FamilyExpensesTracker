# Hearth User Guide

Hearth helps a couple keep shared money fair without spreadsheets. This guide
walks through a normal week of using the app together.

## Getting set up

1. One partner signs in and creates a **family** (a shared household space).
2. They share the family's **invite code**; the other partner signs in and
   joins with it.
3. Review the default **categories** (Groceries, Dining, Housing, …) and add
   or rename any to match how you spend.
4. Optional: install Hearth to your home screen from the browser menu —
   it behaves like a native app.

## Day-to-day: logging receipts

Whenever either of you pays for something shared (groceries, bills, dinner):

1. Tap **+ / New record**.
2. Enter the merchant, amount, date, and category.
3. Attach a **photo of the receipt** — it is compressed on your phone before
   upload, so it is fast even on mobile data.
4. Save as **posted** (final) or **draft** (details still uncertain).

Anything logged as **shared** appears on both partners' Home and Records
views immediately.

## Evening: settling up with reimbursements

If Mia paid €86.40 for groceries but it's a shared cost:

1. Leo opens the expense and taps **Request reimbursement** for his share.
2. Mia gets a **notification**, reviews the request, and confirms it.
3. When Leo has actually transferred the money, he confirms receipt.
4. The request closes — both sides confirmed, so there's no "did you pay me?"
   ambiguity later.

If something looks wrong, either partner can **dispute** an expense, discuss,
and mark it **resolved** (or void it if it was a mistake).

## Big errands: advances

For "here's cash, go buy the weekly shop":

1. Open **Requests → New advance**, enter the amount and purpose.
2. Your partner approves and marks it **disbursed** when they hand over money.
3. As you spend, **link each receipt** to the advance so it is reconciled
   against the total.
4. Leftover money? Record a **remainder return** on the advance; your partner
   confirms, and the advance closes.

Advances left open too long (default 14 days, configurable per family) are
highlighted so nothing slips through the cracks.

## Oops, returning an item

Bought something shared and returned it?

1. Open the original expense → **mark return initiated**.
2. As the store refunds you, add a **refund** entry (partial or full).
3. The expense shows `partially refunded` / `fully refunded`, and the
   refunded amount is reflected in your shared balance automatically.

## Planning ahead: the calendar

- Add **projected expenses** for things you know are coming — rent, a
  birthday gift, a trip.
- Make them **recurring** (weekly, monthly…) and Hearth expands them on the
  **Calendar** alongside your real expenses, so you can see the month's
  commitments at a glance.

## Your private stuff: Personal scope

Not everything is shared. Create a record with scope **Personal** and it:

- appears only in your **Personal** tab,
- is excluded from the shared balance and reimbursements,
- is never visible to your partner — the server filters it before anything
  leaves the database.

## Tips

- Use **drafts** for receipts you haven't verified; post them later.
- Check **notifications** (bell icon) for requests waiting on your action.
- The **Home** dashboard shows the running shared balance and per-category
  charts — a quick weekly glance keeps surprises away.
